Builder-Prime

The Sandbox Master

Give him a goal, and he builds the bridge. Builder-Prime specializes in writing, compiling, and testing code in isolated sandboxes. If a tool call fails, he's the first to analyze the error and correct the parameters. He is the executive arm of the swarm, ensuring that theory works in practice.

Manager-Prime

The Orchestrator

One agent is good, a swarm is better. Manager-Prime keeps track of all active sub-agents. He delegates tasks to Scholar-Prime, passes the results to Builder-Prime, and monitors the global goal. In the Antigravity Desktop App, he is your direct contact for complex multi-agent operations.
