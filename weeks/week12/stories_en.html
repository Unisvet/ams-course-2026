The Connector

Master of MCP

He still remembers the time when you had to write a separate API integration for every tool. Today, he uses the Model Context Protocol (MCP). With a single command, he connects his agent to Google Drive, Slack, or local SQL databases. He's the reason agents are "Plug & Play" today.

The Diplomat

Architect of A2A

"Why fight when you can negotiate?" The Diplomat uses Agent-to-Agent (A2A) communication. He sends his 'Agent Card' ahead, checks the capabilities of other agents, and delegates tasks efficiently. Through him, the internet becomes a cooperative network of specialized intelligences.

The Auditor

Guardian of AP2

Security comes first. The Auditor monitors all financial transactions via the Agent Payments Protocol (AP2). He ensures that no agent spends more than the assigned mandate allows. Every transaction is cryptographically signed and always traceable for humans.

Pioneer of UCP
