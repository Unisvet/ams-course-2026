The Attention Eight

The Collective Behind the Transformer

In summer 2017, a team of eight Google Brain researchers – Ashish Vaswani, Noam Shazeer, Niki Parmar, Jakob Uszkoreit, Llion Jones, Aidan N. Gomez, Łukasz Kaiser, and Illia Polosukhin – published the iconic paper "Attention Is All You Need". All eight authors contributed equally (* equal contribution). By replacing slow sequential processing with parallel self-attention, they unlocked modern Frontier AI. Today, all eight have gone on to found game-changing AI ventures!

Test-Time Compute

Thinking Before Responding

What happens when an AI doesn't just output the first likely token, but pauses to deliberate in a hidden scratchpad? Frontier models leverage "Test-Time Compute": they generate internal reasoning chains (Chain-of-Thought), test their own hypotheses, self-correct bugs, and solve complex mathematical or physical simulation tasks before outputting their final answer.

Agents & MCP

From Chatbots to Autonomous Co-Workers

Frontier AI is no longer limited to chat boxes. Through open standards like the Model Context Protocol (MCP), autonomous agents directly interact with developer tools: executing terminal commands, fetching live simulation data, analyzing JAX computation graphs, and orchestrating multi-agent swarms to solve complex scientific tasks.

The Scaling Frontier

The Bitter Lesson & Emergence

Rich Sutton's famous "Bitter Lesson" proved that raw compute beats human-engineered heuristics. When massive Frontier clusters scaling tens of thousands of TPUs and GPUs train in parallel, emergent abilities spontaneously appear – allowing models to predict protein folding, deduce physical differential equations, and craft bug-free JAX code without explicit task-specific rules.
