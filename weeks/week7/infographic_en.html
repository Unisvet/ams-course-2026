Why are the models so efficient?

Mixture of Experts (MoE)

Classic neural networks (dense models) activate the entire network for every word. MoE models (like Gemini, Grok, or Mixtral) are smarter: they consist of many small "expert networks". A router decides for each input token which 2 or 3 experts are best suited. This saves massive computing power.

Multi-Head Latent Attention (MLA)

Pioneered by systems like DeepSeek and adopted by modern models. MLA solves the memory bottleneck during text generation (KV cache). Instead of time-consumingly storing all keys and values for each word, they are projected into an extremely compact, compressed vector (latent space).

  • ■
    Lower Memory Consumption: Allows context windows of 2 million+ tokens (enough for our entire simulation log files!).
  • ■
    Higher Inference Speed: Models like Grok and Llama can react lightning-fast to system states in our Antigravity ADE (Agent Development Environment).
Result: Economic intelligence. We can perform hundreds of "cognitive calls" per second in our simulation loop without overloading the system.

Short History: On the Shoulders of Giants

2017 - The Foundation

Attention Is All You Need

Google publishes the Transformer paper. The concept of "self-attention" replaces RNNs and enables parallel processing of sequences. The basis of all modern LLMs.

2020 - Scaling Laws

GPT-3 & Scaling Laws

OpenAI proves: The larger the model and the more data, the better the prediction. The era of massive data centers and "few-shot prompting" begins.

2023 - The Open-Weights Era

Llama & The Local Boom

Meta releases the Llama family. Research is democratized. Suddenly, powerful models can run locally on consumer GPUs (or even on laptops through quantization).

2024/2025 - Efficiency & Modality

MoE, DeepSeek & Gemini Multimodal

The focus shifts from pure size to smart architectures (MoE, MLA). Grok and DeepSeek show massive efficiency increases. Gemini establishes true native multimodal understanding (code, image, sound simultaneously).

2026 - The Oracle Awakens

Agentic Era, Claude Opus 4.8 & Gemini 3.5

LLMs are no longer just chatbots. They function as autonomous controllers in system simulations (Antigravity). Claude Opus 4.8 and GPT-5.5 reach unprecedented reliability in autonomous code changes and logical analysis, while Gemini 3.5 Flash offers millisecond latencies for real-time interaction.
