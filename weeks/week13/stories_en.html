🎬 Presentation Fail-Safes & Fun Stories

Behind every great presentation, there is often a story of near-disasters. Here are some "fun stories" and lessons from the world of AI agent presentations.

The Parroting Agent

A student wanted to show how his agent autonomously summarizes a meeting. During the live demo, the agent suddenly started transcribing every sentence of the professor in the room live and commenting with "This is very important!"

Lesson:

Always check microphone permissions and 'always-listening' mode before the demo!

💸

The 500-Dollar Pizza

In a demo for the AP2 protocol, an agent was supposed to order a pizza. Due to an error in the prompt, the agent tried to buy the entire pizzeria because "I'm hungry" was interpreted as "max_priority_buy".

Lesson:

ALWAYS set strict budget guardrails in the demo environment before you press 'Run'.

🐌

The Slow Thinker

A team presented a complex multi-agent system. The agent was so busy with 'reasoning' that it took 5 minutes for an answer. The silence in the room was so loud you could hear the clock ticking.

Lesson:

Use streaming (AG-UI) and 'thinking' indicators so the audience sees that something is happening in the background.

🌌

The Gherkin Hallucination

During the presentation of Spreeland Logistics, the agent claimed steadfastly that gherkins grow in space and that we therefore need rockets for transport to Cottbus.

Lesson:

Grounding is everything! Link your agent via MCP to real databases instead of letting it guess.

✨ How to make a "WOW" presentation?

Visuals Matter

No text deserts! Use diagrams (Mermaid), generated concept arts, and live demos instead of screenshots.

The Story Arc

Start with a pain point (problem), show the path to the solution, and end with a measurable impact.

Live is King

Nothing beats a working live demo. But always have a backup video ready just in case!
