Delegation instead of Dictation

The New Workflow

In the past, we had to debug every line of code ourselves. Today, we simply tell the agent: "Here is the goal, build me the system." The agent in the ADK acts as a junior developer who understands your instructions, recognizes errors on its own, and doesn't stop until the goal is achieved. This is the true power of agentic engineering.

💾

The Agent's Memory

State Management

Why does ChatGPT often forget what we said five minutes ago? Because standard LLMs are stateless. The ADK changes that: it maintains a permanent memory object. Every action, every error message, and every thought is saved so the agent learns from its own mistakes – just like we do.

🌐

Visualizing Thoughts

ADK Web Interface

With `uv run adk web`, you open a window into your agent's mind. You see not just the final result, but the entire "Chain of Thought." Why did the agent choose this tool? Why did it fix this code? Transparency is the key to trustworthy AI.

🐚

Safety First

The Sandbox Environment

Autonomous agents writing code? That sounds dangerous! That's why the ADK executes all operations in a safe sandbox. The agent can experiment, install packages, and test scripts without risking your main system. Freedom for the AI, safety for you.
