Part B: Mastering JAX – Cyber-Magic Academy Manual

The Cyber-Magic Academy Manual provides a hands-on, deep introduction to the JAX ecosystem. It explains why Python needs a compiler like XLA for massive simulations and how JAX utilizes functional purity, stateless arrays, and explicit random keys (PRNGs) to scale calculations on GPUs and TPUs.

Core Topics
  • ◆ Memory Wall, GIL Limits & JAX JIT/XLA Compiler
  • ◆ Functional Purity & Immutable Arrays
  • ◆ Explicit PRNG key splitting (Threefry)
  • ◆ JAX Transformations: grad, jit, vmap & pmap
