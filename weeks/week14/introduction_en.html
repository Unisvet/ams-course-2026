Week 14: Project Presentations (Showcase)

Welcome to the grand finale! This week belongs entirely to you. Over the past weeks, you have learned the tools and methods of Agentic Engineering and System Simulation. Today, you will present your hybrid solutions.

Each student will present their cognitive agent or multi-agent system. We will pay special attention to the logical depth of reasoning, the robustness of tool connections, and the quality of the physical or economic simulation.

✨ Beautiful Presentations

The "Live" Factor

Don't just show code. Show how the agent thinks. Use verbose mode to visualize the reasoning live.

Visual Storytelling

Use clear architecture diagrams (Mermaid/Excalidraw). Less text, more system graphics.

The "Aha" Moment

Highlight a specific situation where your agent made a complex decision autonomously.
