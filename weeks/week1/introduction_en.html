Week 1: The Neo-Simulacrum and the Agentic Stack

In this first week, we lay the foundation of the Neo-Simulacrum. We undergo the paradigm shift from static scripts to autonomous cognitive agents, examine continuous physical systems, and analyze the limits of time-discrete computations.

🎯 Learning Objectives of the Week

  • 1. Understand the transition from classical GUIs (Simulink) to agent-based ReAct workflows.
  • 2. Grasp mathematical modeling of continuous systems and their numerical discretization (scipy.integrate.solve_ivp).
  • 3. Comprehend the importance of dimensionless variables for dimensionality reduction and PINN training.
  • 4. Internalize the architecture of the two-stage Agentic Stack (Layer 1: Deterministic Engine, Layer 2: Cognitive Orchestration).
