⚡ Fourier Neural Operator (FNO)

While PINNs must be completely retrained for each new initial condition, a **Neural Operator** learns a mapping between entire function spaces.

The FNO uses the **Fast Fourier Transform (FFT)**. In the frequency domain, the complex integral-differential equations of physics become simple linear filters.

Zero-Shot Super-Resolution:

Since frequency filtering is independent of grid resolution, the FNO can be trained on coarse grids (16 pts) and evaluated directly on extremely fine grids (128 pts). No recalculation needed!

Measurement Data Integration ❌ Extremely Hard ❌ Hard ✔️ Seamless (as Data Loss) ✔️ Seamless (Pure Data Models)
